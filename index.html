Win % is the backtested chance price hits this level's take-profit before its stop, after filling at the limit. With a 1:6 target you only need about 14% to break even, so green means it has paid over time. Sizes risk about $200.

What the backtest says

Every setup

How often each setup hit its take-profit before its stop, what it needed to break even, and the average result per trade in R ($200 = 1R).

Partials vs holding 10R

Average R per trade if every fill was closed at 2R, 5R or 10R, or a third at each.

Does it hold up over time?

Average R per trade at the take-profit, in two halves of the data. A real edge shows up in both.
